# Domain names

Availability changes daily, so treat everything here as a shortlist to check, not a guarantee. Check on Namecheap or Cloudflare Registrar, and buy the `.in` alongside anything you take for the Indian market.

## The single strongest option

**reallives.world**

You already own reallivesworld.com, so this reads as the same brand with the words separated properly. It is short, memorable on a phone call, easy to say to a principal, and the `.world` extension is on brand rather than a compromise. Run the programme at `journeys.reallives.world` and keep the foundation at the apex.

If it is taken or overpriced, the next best is **changemakerjourney.org**.

## Tier 1: the programme itself

| Domain | Why it works | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| `reallives.world` | Short, on brand, ties to the existing site | Premium pricing is possible on `.world` |
| `changemakerjourney.org` | Says exactly what it is, `.org` reads non profit | Singular is stronger than plural here |
| `thechangemakerjourney.com` | Exact programme name | Long to type |
| `realworldjourney.org` | Carries the RealLives to RealWorld arc | Slightly generic on its own |
| `changemakerjourneys.in` | India first, cheap, easy to defend | Weaker outside India |

## Tier 2: the method, for when you sell to schools

These are the ones that make a principal curious, because they describe the pedagogy rather than the trip.

| Domain | The idea behind it |
|---|---|
| `realworldlabs.org` | The RealWorld Experiential Lab is the actual product |
| `cityasclassroom.com` | The one line that explains the whole method |
| `decodeanation.com` | What the six lenses do |
| `readtheworld.org` | The lens students are handed |
| `fieldworknotsightseeing.com` | Memorable, and it is already your own sentence |

## Tier 3: campaign domains

Short, emotional, and disposable. Point them at a landing page, put them on a poster, retire them when the campaign ends.

| Domain | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `whynotus.org` | "If they could do it, why not us, why not me" |
| `whynotme.in` | The student facing half of the same line |
| `startedatfourteen.com` | "What will you wish your child had started building at fourteen?" |
| `lessonslived.com` | "Some lessons have to be lived" |

`whynotus.org` is the best of these by some distance. It is the emotional core of the whole programme in three words, it works as a hashtag, and students will say it to each other.

## Short links

`rl2rw.com` or `rlrw.in` for WhatsApp forwards, QR codes on the brochure, and the back of a business card. Cheap insurance.

## What to buy now

1. `reallives.world` as the home for everything.
2. `whynotus.org` for the campaign, redirected to the site until you need it.
3. `changemakerjourney.org` defensively, redirected to the site.
4. The `.in` of whichever you make primary.

Around four thousand rupees a year in total, and it closes the door on anyone else taking the obvious names once this starts getting attention.

## Naming advice worth following

Keep the programme name and the domain identical. "RealLives to RealWorld" is a strong programme name but a poor domain, because nobody types six words correctly. The fix is the one above: keep the long name on the brochure, and give the site a short address you can say out loud.
